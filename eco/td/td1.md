## Limits & Critics of the Text

1. **Neoclassical critic**: moving external peoples on the compagnies board move it away of pure profit and so create inefficiences
2. **Path Dependance**: Replicating the German co-determination model is impossible in a lot of country, just like France
3. **Operational Risks**: The threat of greenwashing & reporting fatigue (bureaucratization issues with ESG, *studies from MIT*).
