## 4-Day Workweek

### How does the four-day workweek benefit both employers & employees?

Four day workweek benefits to worker's mental & physic health. A 6 month study of four days workweek in the UK showed that workers from all types of businesses could benefit from less hours of work (with the same wages) from their workers. Data shows that having more time to rest, exercise or with their family helped reducing burnout rate, lower work related stress and showed a global higher job satisfaction. But where do employers benefits from this? Well, workers also reported being less fatigued and are working faster. From the third of the organisation (that accepted to share their results), their revenue increased by an average of 1.4%. Over all it's a human and financial gain ado for corporations, which explains why 56 of the 61 firms which participated in this tests kept those four day workweek.
